## 3. App Description

```text
Welcome to The Arc School official Student & Parent mobile application. Designed to provide a seamless and connected school experience, the app empowers students and parents to stay on top of daily academic activities, schedules, announcements, and performance.

KEY FEATURES:

📅 Interactive Timetable & Class Schedule
• Access your daily and weekly class schedules anytime.
• View subject timings, teachers, and classroom locations at a glance.

📊 Exam Results & Academic Performance
• View detailed term exams, assessments, and grade cards.
• Track your academic progress and subject-wise score breakdowns.

📝 Coursework, Homework & Assignments
• Stay up to date with daily homework and project assignments.
• View deadlines, download study materials, and track completion status.

📢 School Circulars & Announcements
• Never miss important school notifications, circulars, or holiday notices.
• Receive real-time updates and daily thought highlights.

✅ Attendance & Leave Tracking
• Monitor daily attendance records and subject attendance percentages.
• Stay informed on academic compliance and active term days.

🖼️ Event Gallery & School Life
• Explore moments and activities from school events, sports, and cultural festivals.

🔒 Safe, Secure & Private
• Secure individual student authentication.
• Protected access to student personal records and academic data.

Stay organized, stay ahead, and make learning more enjoyable with The Arc School Student App!
```
